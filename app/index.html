The road to platinum

  1. Phase 1 — Play the story

    Take the campaign at your own pace. Twelve trophies ride along with the chapters and named missions, the finale is worth a gold, and the credits unlock the 100% checklist that organises everything after. Sample each of the nine activity types once on the way — Master of None is progress you barely have to think about.

  2. Phase 2 — Close every stranger's story

    Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret each end their strand with a trophy of their own, and any eight finished strands bank the Strangers & Freaks silver. Do these before the big collectible sweep: their missions double as a guided tour of the map you are about to comb.

  3. Phase 3 — Counters, crime and collections

    The honest grind: 250 stunts, gold in all 42 shooting-range challenges, 100 WAiNK scans, 50 challenges on the way to 70, 3 card sets on the way to 15, every meditation spot, and the crime and money trophies. Sweep region by region so one drive serves three lists at once.

  4. Phase 4 — The 100% lap

    Work the in-game checklist category by category until A Long Road turns gold, then mop up whichever single counter is still a few short. The platinum, Vice Got a Price!, pops by itself the moment the 54th other trophy lands. Afterwards: a cocktail on Ocean Beach. There is a trophy for that too.

Missables — the honest version

No trophy on this list is permanently missable: story missions cannot be skipped, strands wait for you, and free roam stays open after the credits. One caution is worth a habit anyway — Actions Speak Louder is tied to story choices and its exact trigger is still being pinned down, so keep a manual save before major decisions and never mash through a choice prompt. And five hidden trophies still have no community-confirmed unlock method; their entries in this guide say so plainly instead of inventing one.

About this list

The 55 names, descriptions and grades here are the official PlayStation 5 trophy list for Grand Theft Auto VI, cross-checked on 20 November 2026 against the published lists at Push Square, KeenGamer, GamesFuze and AlcastHQ: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze. One published guide lists Maximum Withdrawal as bronze; it is silver, as the official distribution confirms, and this guide follows the official list. The how-to text under every trophy is written for this guide and says what it is based on; where the community has not confirmed a hidden trophy's unlock method yet, the entry says so instead of guessing, and the in-game description remains the final word.
